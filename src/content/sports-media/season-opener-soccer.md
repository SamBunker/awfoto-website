Opening day has a feeling you can't fake. The grass is freshly cut, the kits are
spotless, and everyone believes this is the year.

## The Story of the Day

I focused on the connection between teammates — the celebrations, the encouraging
words after a missed chance, the unity of a group still figuring out who it can
become over a long season.

> Documenting a season opener is documenting potential. Anything is still
> possible at zero–zero.

Plenty of football left to play, but the foundation looked strong.
