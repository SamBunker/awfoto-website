## Reading the Game

Volleyball moves fast. To cover it well you learn the rhythm of each rotation and
anticipate where the next play breaks open — the slide attack, the block at the
net, the dig that keeps a rally alive.

- Long rallies that swung momentum point to point
- A net battle that never gave either side an inch
- A finish that came down to the final exchanges

These are the nights that make sports media worth it: high stakes, raw emotion,
and a story that writes itself if you're ready for it.
