Shooting at night forces you to think differently about light. Instead of fighting
the dark, you let it shape the frame and use every available source — headlights,
signage, the glow of a crosswalk signal.

## The Approach

This piece leaned into long exposures and deliberate camera movement to turn
passing traffic into rivers of light. The edit keeps the pace slow on purpose,
letting each shot breathe.

- Practical light sources only — no added rigs
- Slow, intentional moves to stretch the light trails
- A sound design built around the hum of the city

The result is a short, moody study that feels more like a memory of a place than
a literal record of it.
