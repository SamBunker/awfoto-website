A hype reel has one job: make you feel something in under a minute. Every cut,
every beat drop, every freeze frame is in service of that single goal.

## Building the Energy

I start with the audio and edit to the music, not the other way around. The best
moments land on the beat, and the tension builds with the track until the final
release.

> Pacing is everything. A hype reel that peaks too early has nowhere left to go.

Played on the video board before the match, it did exactly what it was supposed
to do — the gym got loud.
